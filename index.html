2. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Memovo collects, processes, and protects your personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data protection laws.

3. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email and password when creating an account.
  • Journal Entries: Texts, photos, and mood ratings you choose to store.
  • Technical Data: Device type, OS version, and anonymized usage metrics for debugging and performance improvements.
  • Subscription Data: Information related to your purchases or trial status (handled by RevenueCat).

4. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

We process your personal data in accordance with Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of contract) and Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). Processing serves to provide you with app functionality, including journaling, cloud sync, AI summaries, and optional voice transcription.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your entries and media are securely stored on Supabase servers located in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase acts as a GDPR-compliant data processor. Data in transit and at rest is encrypted using modern standards (TLS 1.2+ / AES-256). Access is limited to authorized system components and protected by strict access controls.
